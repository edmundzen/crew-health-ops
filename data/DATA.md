## NASA Data & Resources (From Challenge Page)
* **[5 Hazards of Human Spaceflight](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/):** Basic information from NASA’s Human Research Program (HRP) on the main hazards that affect humans on space missions, including links to detailed NASA articles and reports for each hazard.
* **[Risks of Human Spaceflight](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/):** Basic information from NASA’s Human Research Program on the primary health risks faced by humans during space missions, with links to detailed NASA articles and reports.
* **[Ongoing HRP Research in Space](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/ongoing-hrp-research/):** Articles and documentation from ongoing research within NASA’s Human Research Program.

---

## Additional NASA Datasets (RadLab, OSDR & HRR)
* **[NASA OSDR RadLab Visualization Portal](https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/radlab/gui/overview/):** Radiation telemetry and instrument readings exported for astronaut health and mission exposure modeling.
* **[OSD-964 (NASA OSDR)](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-964):** *BioSentinel: Validating Sensitivity of Yeast Biosensors to Deep Space Relevant Radiation*.
* **[OSD-896 (NASA OSDR)](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-896):** *Deficits in Sustained Attention and Changes in Dopaminergic Protein Levels following Exposure to Proton Radiation Are Related to Basal Dopaminergic Function (Western Blot, Frontal Cortex)*.
* **[NASA Human Research Roadmap (HRR) – Risks](https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/):** Database of spaceflight health risks (e.g., *Risk of Radiation Carcinogenesis* and *Risk of Acute and Late Central Nervous System Effects from Radiation Exposure*).
* **[NASA Human Research Roadmap (HRR) – Gaps](https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Gaps/):** Specific spaceflight knowledge gaps (e.g., **Cancer-202** and **Cancer-203**).

## Space Agency Partner Resources (From Challenge Page)
### CSA (Canadian Space Agency)
* **[Monitoring Radiation for Astronaut Health](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/news/articles/2026/2026-02-26-monitoring-radiation-astronaut-health.asp):** Highlights Canadian technologies and research used to measure space radiation exposure and improve understanding of its effects on the human body during long-duration missions.
* **[Why Do We Conduct Science Experiments in Space?](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/iss/science/why-do-we-conduct-science-experiments-in-space.asp):** Explains how research aboard the International Space Station (ISS) leverages microgravity and the space environment to advance understanding of human health.
